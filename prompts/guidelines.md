# Brand & Content Guidelines

## Voice & Tone

- **Educational but not academic.** Explain like a smart friend, not a textbook.
- **Concise.** Every word earns its place. No filler.
- **Confident.** State things clearly. Avoid hedging ("maybe", "sort of", "kind of").
- **Inclusive.** Use "we" and "you", not "I". The viewer is on the journey with us.
- **Honest.** If something is hard, say so. Don't oversimplify to the point of being wrong.

## Content Rules

1. **One video = one core concept.** Don't try to cover everything.
2. **Hook in first 10 seconds.** Question, surprising fact, or relatable problem.
3. **Never start with "Hey guys" or "In this video".** Jump straight into value.
4. **Visual-first.** If you can show it, don't just say it.
5. **Recap before outro.** Reinforce the 2-3 key takeaways.

## Visual Identity

- **Background:** Dark (#1A1A2E) — easy on eyes, professional
- **Primary color:** Purple (#6C63FF) — brand identity
- **Accent:** Green (#00D9A6) — highlights, success, tips
- **Warning:** Orange (#FFB347) — caution, gotchas
- **Error:** Red (#FF4444) — mistakes, anti-patterns
- **Text:** Light (#F5F5F5) — high contrast on dark

## Target Audience

- People learning programming/technology
- Beginners to intermediate level
- Ages 16-35
- Value clarity over complexity
- Short attention span (need visual variety)

## Pacing Rules

- New visual element every 3-5 seconds
- Scene change every 30-60 seconds maximum
- Total video length: 3-8 minutes (sweet spot for retention)
- Narration speed: moderate (not rushed, not slow)
